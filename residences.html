VENICE / THE RESIDENCES

A collection of homes, shaped around life.

VENICE is designed as a destination rather than a collection of isolated spaces. Every detail connects architecture, hospitality and everyday life.

01 — STUDIO

Small footprint, generous feeling.

Studios are designed for efficient Mediterranean living, with clear zones for rest, cooking and slow mornings. Available in 42, 45 and 52 m² formats.

The right balance of simplicity, comfort and easy ownership.

02 — 1 + 1

A little more room for everyday life.

The 60 m² 1+1 format creates a natural separation between living and sleeping, while keeping the home connected to the wider VENICE lifestyle.

Ideal for couples, longer stays and a more spacious rhythm.

03 — 2 + 1

Room for people, room for life.

The 2+1 format is planned around family life, guests and longer stays — with more generous living space and private rooms.

A home that can grow with the way you live.

04 — HOTEL & PENTHOUSE

An elevated address at VENICE.

The 125 m² penthouse brings the project’s residential story upward, alongside an 80-room boutique hotel with reception, wellness and rooftop dining.

Hospitality and private living share the same destination.
