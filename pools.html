VENICE / 01 / EXPERIENCE

Water shapes the destination.

VENICE is designed as a destination rather than a collection of isolated spaces. Every detail connects architecture, hospitality and everyday life.

01 — THE CANAL

A 250 m Venetian gesture.

The VENICE Canal is conceived as a defining landscape element, with the possibility of gondola rides and a slower way to move through the project.

02 — THE MAIN POOL

400 m² of open-air water.

A generous swimming pool forms the centre of the outdoor leisure experience.

03 — AQUAPARKS

Energy for every age.

Separate aquaparks for adults and children bring a more playful side to the destination.

04 — INDOOR WATER

A year-round rhythm.

An indoor swimming pool extends the wellness experience beyond the warmer months.

05 — POOL BAR

Stay a little longer.

The pool bar keeps refreshments close to the water and the social heart of the resort.
